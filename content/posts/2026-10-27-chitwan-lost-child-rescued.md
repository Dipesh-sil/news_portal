A 3-year-old toddler who had wandered away from home on Tuesday morning has been safely found and reunited with his family within six hours, following a swift community search.

The young child had walked toward the local marketplace while playing outside. An alert local shopkeeper noticed the unaccompanied toddler, provided immediate care, and informed the nearby police station.

### Quick Facts:
- **Location:** Chitwan, Nepal
- **Search Duration:** 6 hours from disappearance report
- **Current Status:** Child is healthy and back safely with parents
- **Police Reminder:** Parents are reminded to keep a close eye on young children around busy roads and market streets.

मंगलबार बिहान घर नजिकै खेल्दाखेल्दै हराएका ३ वर्षीय बालक ६ घण्टाभित्र सकुशल फेला परेका छन्। स्थानीय बजार क्षेत्रमा एक्लै हिँडिरहेको देखेपछि स्थानीय पसलेले प्रहरीलाई खबर गरेका थिए।

प्रहरीको सक्रियता र स्थानीयको सहयोगमा बालकको पहिचान खुलेपछि सकुशल आमाबुबाको जिम्मा लगाइएको छ।
